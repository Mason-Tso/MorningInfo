GROWTH EQUITY 101: A venture fund isn't the general partner's [GP, the firm running the fund] own money; it mostly comes from limited partners [LPs], institutions like pensions and endowments that commit capital for years. The GP charges LPs a management fee, typically around 2% of the fund a year, and keeps carry, usually 20% of profits, only if the fund actually makes money. That structure is why Insight's Deven Parekh argued for spreading bets across many companies rather than one or two: a GP manages risk for LPs locked in long-term, not the single best-looking deal.
