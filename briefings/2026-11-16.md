GROWTH EQUITY 101: CesiumAstro's round is a good way to learn how growth equity differs from venture capital and from a buyout. Venture capital backs young, often unprofitable companies on a bet; a buyout firm buys most of a mature company and runs it directly; growth equity sits in between, writing a big check for a minority stake in a company with real revenue, like CesiumAstro, without taking control. Janus Henderson's Ascent platform exists for exactly that step: funding a company once it has outgrown venture rounds but isn't ready to go public. Asking an investor whether their firm does venture, growth, or buyouts is a fast way to learn how they think about risk.

LEARN ONE THING

Today's bank news is a good way to learn the difference between a stablecoin and a tokenized deposit. A stablecoin, like USDC, is a token anyone can hold outside the banking system, backed by reserves the issuer holds. A tokenized deposit is just a bank deposit with a blockchain wrapper: it only moves between accounts at participating banks and disappears if the bank fails, safer for the bank but less portable for the customer. That's why Citi, Goldman and UBS are chasing both at once.
