LEARN ONE THING

A procedural vote in the Senate, sometimes called a cloture vote, decides whether to end debate on a bill so a final vote can happen, not whether the bill itself passes. It usually needs 60 of 100 senators, a much higher bar than the simple majority needed to pass most legislation. That's why Monday's procedural vote on the CLARITY Act crypto bill matters: even if it succeeds, the bill still has to survive negotiation between Democrats and Republicans before it can become law.
