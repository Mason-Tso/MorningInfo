LEARN ONE THING

Coinbase getting approval to run its own derivatives clearinghouse matters more than it sounds, because a clearinghouse sits between every buyer and seller in a trade and guarantees both sides get paid even if one goes bust. That's exactly the job that strained when Situational Awareness, the hedge fund now under SEC scrutiny, got hit with margin calls it couldn't meet fast enough. Whoever runs the clearinghouse controls how much collateral traders must post, which is why owning that piece of infrastructure is valuable enough for Coinbase to build rather than rent.
