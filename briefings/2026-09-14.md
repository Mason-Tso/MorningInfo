GROWTH EQUITY 101: Growth deals like Motive's are usually priced as a multiple of ARR, meaning investors pay some multiple of a company's yearly subscription revenue for their stake, rather than valuing it on profit like a mature company. A fast, efficient software company might trade for 10 times ARR or more, while a slower one trades for much less, which is why growth investors obsess over the growth rate, not just the revenue level. Motive's $600 million in ARR growing 30% a year is exactly the number that lets it raise at a rich price instead of rushing to go public.

LEARN ONE THING

Four times a year, including this Wednesday, the Fed releases the dot plot, a chart where every official anonymously marks where they think interest rates will be over the next few years. It matters because it shows how divided or aligned officials really are, not just what the Fed decides that day. If Wednesday's dots show more hikes ahead, that would rattle markets even more than the hike itself, since it would mean a new tightening cycle rather than a one-off move.
