LEARN ONE THING

A tokenized deposit and a stablecoin can look similar, both are digital tokens representing dollars, but they're legally different. A tokenized deposit is still a bank deposit, insured and regulated like a checking account, just represented as a token that moves instantly on a blockchain. A stablecoin like USDC isn't a deposit at all; it's more like a prepaid instrument backed by reserves the issuer holds. That's why JPMorgan, Citi and Bank of America are racing to build their own tokenized-deposit network instead of just accepting stablecoins as the future of digital money.
