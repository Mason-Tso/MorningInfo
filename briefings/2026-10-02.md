LEARN ONE THING

Markets have a "fear gauge" called the VIX, which measures how much volatility options traders expect in stocks over the next 30 days; a low reading, like today's near 16, means traders expect calm, while a reading above 30 usually signals panic. It's odd the VIX is this low while the 10-year Treasury yield sits near a 24-year high, since big bond moves often spill into stocks eventually. Traders watch that gap closely, because a calm VIX during a nervous bond market can mean stock investors are either right that nothing bad is coming, or just not paying attention yet.
