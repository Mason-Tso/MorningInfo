LEARN ONE THING

Forward guidance is when a central bank hints ahead of time which way it's likely to move interest rates, so markets aren't caught off guard. Fed Chair Kevin Warsh has deliberately refused to give any, which is unusual and makes each meeting harder for traders to prepare for, because bets can swing sharply right up until the announcement. That is part of why next Wednesday's decision feels like even more of a coin flip than a typical Fed meeting.
