LEARN ONE THING

Today's inflation report will show two numbers: headline CPI, which includes volatile things like gas and groceries, and core CPI, which strips those out because they swing for reasons unrelated to the economy's underlying trend. The Fed watches core CPI more closely because it is a steadier signal of whether inflation pressure is really building or easing. Since oil has spiked because of the Iran conflict, today's headline number could look worse than the economy actually is, so investors will focus on the core reading instead.
