DECODER

DXY: the U.S. Dollar Index, tracking the dollar's value against a basket of major currencies; up means a stronger dollar.
PPI vs. CPI: PPI measures what producers charge (wholesale inflation, a leading indicator); CPI measures what consumers pay.
Basis point (bp): 1/100th of a percentage point; 25bp = 0.25%.
2s10s curve: the gap between 2-year and 10-year Treasury yields; a widely watched growth/inflation signal.
Tokenized deposit: a bank deposit represented as a digital token, redeemable 1:1, that moves instantly like a stablecoin but stays inside the banking system.
Stablecoin: a cryptocurrency designed to hold a steady value, usually pegged 1:1 to the dollar and backed by cash or short-term Treasuries.
Equity perpetuals: derivative contracts that track a stock's price with no expiration date; common in crypto, new to U.S. equities.
Redemption cap: a limit a fund puts on how much investors can withdraw at once, used to avoid a fire sale of illiquid assets.
