LEARN ONE THING

Today's big number was the Producer Price Index, or PPI, which measures the prices businesses charge each other for goods and materials before those costs reach a store shelf. Tomorrow brings the Consumer Price Index, or CPI, which measures what regular people actually pay at checkout. Economists watch PPI as an early warning sign for CPI, because when it costs businesses more to make or ship something, they usually pass some of that cost on to customers a few months later. That's why today's hot PPI made investors nervous about tomorrow's CPI too.
