GROWTH EQUITY 101: Bessemer's two new funds show the difference between venture capital and growth equity, which often get lumped together. Venture capital (the $1.75 billion fund) bets on brand-new, unproven startups, where most fail and a few big winners pay for everyone else's losses. Growth equity (the $4 billion fund) instead buys minority stakes in companies that already have real customers and revenue, betting on scaling something that already works. That's why growth-equity due diligence [research before investing] leans much harder on a company's actual financials, and it's a good coffee-chat question: how much of your decision comes from the story versus the numbers?

LEARN ONE THING

Today's jump in Treasury yields came partly from a "weak auction," when the government sells new bonds and doesn't get enough buyer demand at the price it wanted. When that happens, the Treasury has to offer a higher rate to attract buyers, which is why the 5-year yield broke above 5% for the first time since 2007. It signals that big bond buyers, like pension funds and foreign governments, want more compensation to hold U.S. debt right now, not just a one-day blip.
