GROWTH EQUITY 101: Fal's round is a good way to learn the Rule of 40, a quick test growth investors use on software and infrastructure companies: add a company's revenue growth rate to its profit margin, and if the sum clears 40, the business is considered healthy. A fast enough grower can pass even while burning cash; a slower one needs real profits to clear the same bar. Fal hasn't disclosed a margin, so investors are betting its growth rate alone makes the math work. Asking an investor whether a pitch is growth-led or margin-led is a fast way to learn which side of the Rule of 40 they're underwriting.
