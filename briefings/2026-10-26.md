LEARN ONE THING

Today's Anthropic story is a good chance to learn "lead-left." Several banks share the underwriting job on a stock offering, but one takes the lead-left spot, named for where its logo prints on the prospectus cover, and runs the roadshow for the biggest cut of fees. Morgan Stanley being the reported frontrunner for that role on what could be the largest IPO in history matters because lead-left on a deal this size is both a trophy and a huge payday, exactly why banks fight so hard over it.
