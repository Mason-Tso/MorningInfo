LEARN ONE THING

Today's Tether lawsuit is a good excuse to learn that a stablecoin is not quite the same as cash in your pocket. Even though $1 of USDT is designed to always be worth $1, the company behind it keeps a list of wallets it can freeze at any time, often to comply with a police investigation or sanctions. That is very different from physical cash, which nobody can switch off once it's in your hand, which is why big institutions building on stablecoins still ask who controls that power.
