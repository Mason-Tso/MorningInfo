LEARN ONE THING

Today's Bank of America story is a good chance to learn about unrealized losses, or paper losses. When a bank buys a bond and rates later rise, that bond's market price falls, since new bonds now pay more to attract buyers. If the bank doesn't sell, it hasn't actually lost money yet, but if it ever needed to sell in a hurry, say to cover a sudden wave of withdrawals, the paper loss becomes a real one instantly, which is exactly what toppled Silicon Valley Bank in 2023.
