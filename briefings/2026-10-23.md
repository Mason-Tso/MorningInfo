A step-up this large and fast is unusual outside the current AI boom, where investors will pay far more for the same company in months if the growth story holds. The opposite, a down round, prices a company lower than last time, a sign investors lost confidence, which is why step-ups get celebrated and down rounds get buried in the fine print. LEARN ONE THING

Today's Anthropic story is a good excuse to learn what a "stabilization agent" does in an IPO. Goldman Sachs is expected to take that role, meaning for a short window after Anthropic starts trading, Goldman can buy back shares in the open market to support the price if it drops too fast. Banks get this power through a greenshoe, an option letting the lead underwriter sell extra shares to cover those purchases, which is why a stock's first few days often look calmer than they'd otherwise be.
