LEARN ONE THING

Binance's hack is a good chance to learn the difference between a hot wallet and cold storage. A hot wallet is connected to the internet so an exchange can move customers' crypto quickly, which makes it convenient but also the part hackers can reach; cold storage keeps the bulk of funds on a device that's never online, safer but slower to access. Exchanges try to keep only a small slice of funds in hot wallets for exactly this reason, and a breach this size suggests something in that safeguard failed. Asking a crypto firm how it splits funds between hot and cold storage is a sharp way to gauge how seriously it takes security.
