LEARN ONE THING

Kraken parent Payward's profit falling sharply even as its business grows is a good way to learn adjusted EBITDA [earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization], the number companies often highlight instead of plain net income. EBITDA strips out costs like debt interest and the paper loss from aging equipment, which supporters say shows the underlying business better, and critics say makes performance look healthier than actual cash profit. That's why asking an investor "is that EBITDA or net income" is a sharp way to see what they're really comparing.
