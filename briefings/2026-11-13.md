GROWTH EQUITY 101: Shield AI's deal is a good way to learn structured and preferred equity, what growth investors often buy instead of plain common stock. Preferred shares typically get paid back before common stockholders if a company is sold or wound down, and pairing that with straight debt lets backers like Blackstone-managed funds limit their downside on a huge bet while still sharing the upside as the valuation climbs. Plain common stock, what most venture rounds buy, has no such protection and is paid last. Asking an investor whether their check was common or structured preferred is a sharp way to learn how much downside protection they actually built in.

LEARN ONE THING

Today's wholesale-prices report is a good way to learn the difference between the PPI and the CPI, the two inflation numbers that move markets most. The CPI, the Consumer Price Index, measures what households actually pay; the PPI, the Producer Price Index released this morning, measures what businesses charge each other further up the supply chain, before it reaches a store shelf. Economists watch the PPI partly because cost increases there often show up in the CPI a month or two later. That is why a hot PPI print would raise the odds the Fed holds rates steady on December 9, even though it is not the inflation number consumers actually feel.
