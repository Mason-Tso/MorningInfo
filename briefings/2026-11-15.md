LEARN ONE THING

Ripple's new charter is a good way to learn what a nationally chartered trust bank is. Unlike a normal bank, a trust bank cannot take deposits or make loans; it can only hold and manage assets on clients' behalf, exactly what a crypto company needs to custody digital assets for institutions. Getting that charter from the OCC, a federal regulator, lets Ripple operate in all 50 states under one license instead of collecting a separate money-transmitter license in each one, which is slower and costlier. That is why crypto and fintech firms increasingly chase this kind of charter instead of becoming a full bank.
