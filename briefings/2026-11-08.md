LEARN ONE THING

Anthropic's IPO is a good way to learn what a stabilization agent does, since Goldman Sachs holds that role on the deal. For a set window after a new stock starts trading, the agent can buy shares in the open market to support the price if it wobbles, using extra shares sometimes called the "greenshoe." It exists because a brand-new stock can be volatile at first, and banks want early buyers to feel the pricing was fair. If Anthropic's stock dips right out of the gate, watch for Goldman stepping in as buyer of last resort.
