GROWTH EQUITY 101: Ramp and Brex are a good pair for learning IPO versus acquisition exits. Brex chose the acquisition route, selling to Capital One this year for about $5 billion, a quick, certain payday but a lower price than its backers once hoped for. LEARN ONE THING

Today's JPMorgan dividend increase is a good chance to learn the difference between a record date and a payment date. Only shareholders who owned the stock by the record date, October 6, get paid, while the cash itself goes out on the payment date, October 31. That gap matters on a trading desk because a stock usually drops by roughly the dividend amount the next trading day, called the ex-dividend date, since new buyers no longer get that payment.
