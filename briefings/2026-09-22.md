LEARN ONE THING

Several of today's stories mention "perpetual futures," a derivatives contract that lets traders bet on an asset's price with borrowed money but, unlike a normal futures contract, never expires. A normal futures contract forces a settlement date; a perpetual just keeps rolling, held near the real market price by a funding rate, a small payment traders on one side of the bet periodically make to the other side. That is why Kraken routing Hyperliquid perpetuals through a CFTC-regulated exchange matters: these contracts have mostly lived on offshore, unregulated platforms, and this would be one of the first legal ways to trade them inside the U.S.
