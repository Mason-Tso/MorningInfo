LEARN ONE THING

A normal stock exchange matches buyers and sellers through an order book, where a trade happens only when someone's buy price meets someone's sell price. An automated market maker, or AMM, works differently: it uses a pool of two assets and a math formula to set the price automatically, so anyone can trade instantly against the pool with no waiting for a match. Uniswap pioneered this model for crypto, and it's exactly the mechanism the SEC's new exemption lets tokenized stocks use, which is why Uniswap's own token jumped on the news. It means a share of Apple could someday trade through the same kind of pool that today only holds crypto tokens.
