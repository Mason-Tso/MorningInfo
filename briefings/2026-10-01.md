GROWTH EQUITY 101: Growth investors usually buy preferred shares, not the common shares employees and founders hold, because preferred stock gets paid back first if the company is ever sold. A tender offer like ElevenLabs' sets a fresh price by having outside investors buy existing shares, but it's worth asking whether common or preferred stock traded, since employees selling common often get a different price than an investor's preferred is marked at. LEARN ONE THING

Today's cooler inflation number came from core PCE, not the CPI report you usually hear about. The Fed officially targets PCE because it adjusts for people switching to cheaper products when prices rise, which economists think gives a more accurate read than CPI's fixed basket of goods. That's why a trader hears "CPI" most days but "PCE" is the number that actually moves what the Fed decides.
