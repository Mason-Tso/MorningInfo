LEARN ONE THING

Today's DTCC story is a good chance to learn what "tokenizing" an asset means. A tokenized stock or bond is legally the same ownership claim as always, just represented as a digital token on a shared ledger instead of a traditional database, letting it settle in minutes instead of the usual two business days. Banks like it because faster settlement means less money tied up while a trade finishes, which is why they are building this plumbing well before most investors notice anything different in their own accounts.
