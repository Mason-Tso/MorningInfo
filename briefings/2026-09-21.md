LEARN ONE THING

Bitcoin's jump above $84,000 today came partly from a "short squeeze." Traders who bet a price will fall can "short" it, essentially borrowing and selling an asset now to buy it back cheaper later. When the price rises instead, those traders must buy back in to cap their losses, and that rush of buying pushes the price up even further, which is what happened when more than $260 million of bearish bitcoin bets got wiped out in an hour today. A sharp price move is not always about new information; sometimes it is just a crowded bet unwinding all at once.
